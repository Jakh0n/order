# Tezda logo: brief and concepts (round 1)

- **Name:** Tezda ("tezda" = quickly), tezda.app. Same product as Flami: a menu on one link, orders in Telegram/WhatsApp, small food businesses in the CIS.
- **Style requested:** "very creative, Apple style". That means one simple iconic shape, no small details, a clean lowercase wordmark.
- **Adjectives:** fast, simple, clean, warm.
- **Avoid:** food clichés (fork and knife, chef hat, scooter, bag, pin), plain speed lines, lightning (42.uz), and Telegram/WhatsApp icons.

## Tests (v2)

All three hold at 32px, in one colour and reversed.
- **Dash-dot:** at 16px the wide shape gets small. The favicon or app icon would have to place it inside a square tile. Risk: it can read like an iOS toggle switch.
- **Bowl T:** can read as a funnel or a lamp.
- **Timer plate:** can read as a pie chart or Pac-Man.

Trademark: not checked. "tezda" is a generic word; check the IMA register.
